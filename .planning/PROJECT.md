# Permit2

## What This Is

Permit2 is a simple, beautiful permit management system designed for small teams and POC demonstrations. It allows managers to create, track, approve, and manage permits through a clean, modern UI. The system is scoped as a proof-of-concept with a focus on visual quality and ease of use.

## Core Value

Managers can issue, track, and approve permits end-to-end through a beautiful, intuitive interface without friction.
